Coastal Climate

The following are the News on several Zanzibar Climate related Issues

Consistent year-round, ranging from 25°C to 35°C. The northern and eastern coasts benefit from steady trade winds, which help moderate the high humidity.

Temperatures

The coolest and driest period. Temperatures average 26°C to 30°C, making it the most popular time for visitors

Long Dry Season (June - October)

The hottest time of the year, with daytime temperatures regularly pushing 32°C to 35°C and low rainfall.

Short Dry Season (January - February)

The wettest months, characterized by heavy, prolonged tropical downpours. The western side of the island tends to see slightly heavier rainfall during this period.
